## 📌 Description
The **Augaton SCP-131 System** is a Garry's Mod addon that lets players be the Eye Pods: small, harmless creatures that roll around the facility and keep SCP-173 under watch.

It runs on the GuthSCP base and is made for SCP Roleplay servers.

> ⚠️ **IMPORTANT**  
> This addon requires [[GuthSCP] Base](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3034737316) and [[GuthSCP] SCP-173](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3034740066).  
> Without them, the addon will **not function correctly**.

---

## 🔧 Features
- 👁️ Playable SCP-131-A and SCP-131-B, each with its own skin
- 🛞 Wheel movement: acceleration, coasting, braking and less grip at top speed
- 🧗 Climbs walls while the jump key is held, and hops over the ledge at the top
- 💥 Gets stunned when it crashes into a wall at high speed
- 🐾 Bonds with a player when secondary fire is held on them; the bond breaks if they stop paying attention to it
- 🗿 Holds SCP-173 in place with its unblinking eye, until it gets stunned
- 🐝 Speed bonus when both pods roll together
- 🕊️ Harmless and immortal by default, as a Safe-class SCP
- 🔑 Optional keycard level with the GuthSCP keycard module
- ⚙️ Configurable in game through GuthSCP: movement, climbing, bonding, sounds and more

---

## 📥 Installation
1. Subscribe to the addon on the Steam Workshop
2. Subscribe to [GuthSCP] Base and [GuthSCP] SCP-173
3. Restart your server or game
4. Configure the module from the GuthSCP menu
